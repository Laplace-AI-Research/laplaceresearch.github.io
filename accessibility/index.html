Accessibility

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA across this site.

Charts come with tables or text that carry the same numbers. Every control works from the keyboard and shows a visible focus outline, and animation is turned off when your system asks for reduced motion.

If something isn’t accessible to you, email inquiries@laplaceresearch.org and we’ll fix it. Please tell us the page and what got in the way.
