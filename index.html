Introducing Laplace Oracle: Our family of models that buy evidence before they forecast

Six open models trained to buy observations and report probabilities in synthetic worlds. Every one declined a copied reading, yet every one changed its forecast when that reading was supplied anyway.

When thinking helps models believe forged records

In a paired experiment on four open models, thinking mode raised final-answer accuracy by 10 to 34 points and made most models harder to pressure, but every model became worse at rejecting a record pasted into the chat.

Research noteNovember 27, 2026

Signet: open models that check where a record came from before they believe it

Most models accept a fake record pasted into the chat as readily as a genuine tool result. Signet-2B, 4B and 9B adopt genuine records and reject pasted ones, including in formats they never saw in training, and still accept valid corrections.
