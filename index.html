Laplace Research

Foundation models for calibrated forecasting

Laplace’s mission is to build models that reason under uncertainty well enough to forecast the world. To do so, we focus on solving two core problems:

  • Aggregating resolved forecasting data across markets, history, and expert judgment
  • Understanding the internal mechanisms that produce calibrated belief

Each step forward on these axes tightens the loop between prediction and interpretation: better data sharpens calibration, and understanding why a model is calibrated tells us what data to collect next.

We’re a small team working out of the Bay Area. If you’re working on forecasting, calibration, or interpretability, reach out.
