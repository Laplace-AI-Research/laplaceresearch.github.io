Foundation models for calibrated forecasting

A forecast is only useful if you know how much to trust it. We build forecasting models whose confidence can be taken at face value, and study the mechanisms that make it so. Every release ships with its data, code, and limits.

Agenda

Two problems that tighten each other

Better data sharpens calibration, and understanding why a model is calibrated tells us which data to collect next.

Forecasting data

We aggregate resolved forecasting data across markets, history, and expert judgment. A model can only learn calibrated confidence from outcomes that are well specified and consistently resolved.

Mechanisms of calibrated belief

We study the internal mechanisms that produce calibrated predictions, and how a model’s beliefs should move when evidence arrives, so we can tell where its confidence should hold and where it should fail.
