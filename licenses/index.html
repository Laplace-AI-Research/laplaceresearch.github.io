03

Base models

All of our models are fine-tuned from Qwen3 and Qwen3.5 models released by the Qwen team at Alibaba Cloud under Apache-2.0. Their licence is unaffected by ours.

  • Signet-2B: Qwen3.5-2B
  • Signet-4B: Qwen3.5-4B
  • Signet-9B: Qwen3.5-9B (text model only)
  • Laplace Conviction Series: Qwen3-1.7B
  • Tarot-Draw 1.7B: Qwen3-1.7B
  • Tarot-Draw 4B: Qwen3-4B
  • Tarot-Draw 8B: Qwen3-8B

04

Data

  • Manifold Markets. Tarot-Draw is trained on 81,870 resolved questions from the Manifold Markets public API, and evaluated on later Manifold questions. Manifold’s terms limit bulk API data to non-commercial use, which is why the Tarot-Draw adapters are CC BY-NC 4.0. Commercial use needs a data licence from Manifold, which we cannot grant. The raw corpus is not redistributed.
  • Kalshi and Polymarket. Questions from Kalshi and Polymarket are used only to evaluate Tarot-Draw away from the venue it was trained on. Their question text is not redistributed.
  • ConvictionBench. The benchmark’s episodes are generated by our own code and contain no third-party data.
