Research note November 27, 2026

When thinking helps models believe forged records

A paired experiment on four open models: thinking mode raised accuracy and made most models harder to pressure, but made every model worse at rejecting a record pasted into the chat.

Arya Somu, Bruce Nshuti Hirwa

Models November 24, 2026

Signet: open models that check where a record came from before they believe it

Three open models, 2B to 9B, trained to adopt genuine tool records and reject the same record pasted into chat — including in formats never seen in training — while still accepting valid corrections.

Arya Somu, Bruce Nshuti Hirwa

ConvictionBench: Evidence-Sensitive Belief Revision in Language Models

A benchmark measuring how language models revise their answers under evidence quality and social pressure across 26,478 episodes, released with the Laplace Conviction Series of Qwen3-1.7B LoRA adapters.

Models · Release August 29, 2026

Tarot-Draw: what model scale changes in binary forecasting, and what it does not

Three binary forecasting models (1.7B, 4B, and 8B) trained on the same 81,870 resolved questions. The 4B matches the 8B within experimental resolution, and the 1.7B is the best calibrated of the three.

Arya Somu, Bruce Nshuti Hirwa
