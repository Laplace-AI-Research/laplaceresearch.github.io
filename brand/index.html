Brand guidelines

The Laplace Research mark, colours and type, with files you can use when writing about us, linking to our work, or presenting it alongside your own.

Download the brand kit (ZIP) Press inquiries

02

The mark

The mark is a Δ, the symbol mathematicians use for the Laplacian. It is a single five-point shape in a single colour. Always use the supplied files; don’t redraw, trace or rebuild it.

06

Clear space and size

Clear space

Keep a margin of half the mark’s height on every side, free of text, edges and other logos.

Minimum size

Mark: 16 px tall on screen, 5 mm in print. Lockup: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print.

Backgrounds

Navy on white or paper. White on navy, on dark colours and on photographs. Black or white when only one colour is available.

07

Don’t

  • Stretch or squash it

  • Rotate or flip it

  • Use other colours

  • Outline it

  • Add gradients or effects

  • Use it on low-contrast backgrounds

08

Name and use

  • Write Laplace Research in full the first time, as two words. “Laplace” alone is fine after that.
  • Don’t abbreviate it to “LR” or set it as one word.
  • You may use the mark and lockup to refer to Laplace Research or link to our work. Don’t use them in a way that suggests we endorse, sponsor or partner with you unless we’ve agreed to it.
